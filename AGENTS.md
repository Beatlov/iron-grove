# Project instructions

Desktop-mouse, single-player military space pixel strategy. User-facing text is Russian. Humans versus technological demons in space armor, on ruined industrial planets. Keep the H3 olive/sand humans, graphite/red demons, bone trim, green/amber indicators and coarse native pixel clusters.

- Offline operation and file:// support; no build tools, dependencies, remote assets/fonts or telemetry. Preview: `python3 serve.py 8000` from any cwd. Keep HTML asset query versions and visible HELIOS build stamp in sync.
- Simulation in engine.js is DOM-independent, exported through window.RTS and CommonJS. art.js must never mutate simulation. UI and scenes belong in game.js.
- H4 has 12 space regions, each a separate planet surface of 144×96 tiles. Local maps have no regional subdivision or capture outposts. Only owned surfaces may be opened; foreign planets expose status and expedition targets.
- Food, tokens, minerals, crystals, gas, sulfur, weapons, ammo, armor, explosives, medicines, herbs, mycelium and psiomycin physically occupy inventories of bases (5000 shared capacity), warehouses (5000), and production hubs (240). Team stock is an aggregate view over ready stores, excluding all reserved military supplies, never an independent duplicate ledger. Costs and upkeep use the shared total; military orders reserve every required material in real local-store inventory until pickup. Carriers must retain loads when local stores are full. Captures transfer inventories; destroyed stores lose goods. Population is global: residents + military/training + six reserved places per colony mission. Do not duplicate counts when units travel or enter battle.
- Residents are humans, never manually commanded, automatically build/repair and collect raw minerals/crystals; ordinary logistics belongs exclusively to docked robots in H22. Idle residents hide inside the core, commute visibly to assigned work, and return after release/construction. Farmers visibly sow/harvest fields and return carrying crops; hide only inside the farm while processing/waiting. Humans consume food only, never tokens. Hire them for 20 tokens at a base in a sequential 8-second queue (max 5), reserving population capacity immediately. Staffing is controlled by building cards. Stationary workers hide indoors; farmers sow fields (0.6 worker-seconds), grow crops (6 seconds), harvest exactly 4 per cycle (0.6 seconds) and process for 0.4 seconds. No new sowing until growth and all harvesting are finished; early harvest must preserve the fixed cycle yield. No automatic births or converting residents into soldiers.
- H20 extraction: ironmine/goldmine are outdoor collection posts (2 slots, matching unexhausted source within 8 center-to-center tiles, no deposit overlap). Metal load6/gather3s; crystals load4/gather3.5s; unload .75s. Preserve exclusive source claims, raw cargo and carrier delivery from buffer60. Gas/sulfur stations must cover their deposit; 2 indoor workers, rates .75/.6 each, finite extraction only inside footprint. Source of truth is stats.collector/radius/load/gatherTime/packTime versus stats.extractor/rate, not old GAS_* compatibility exports. Tiers remain metadata. H26 weapons recipe: 9 minerals + 3 gas / 12s.
- Inactive military units exist only in campaign rosters; H25 deployment creates temporary real surface.entities objects shared with battle.units, then removes them at finish without population duplication. Train using class-specific supplies (weapons/ammo/armor; medicines/armor; explosives/armor), tokens and capacity. Training waits for physical delivery of all resources. Fleets carry chosen counts. H21 battles bind actual 144×96 planet surfaces, or persistent smaller site surfaces, with automatic attacks/healing/kiting and no manual orders. Supply shortages slowly remove individual military units.
- Unguarded worlds are captured after travel; default neutral worlds require a confirmed battle victory. Colonization costs resources and six free slots, then creates a base and six new residents. Enemy victory transfers damaged buildings and part of the civilian population, preserving surviving workplace assignments. Residents repair automatically for minerals.
- H6 uses exact grid-aligned footprints: 4×4 base/factory/barracks, 3×3 house/warehouse/farm/cryptofarm, 2×2 extraction/field/tower. Rendering, collision, preview and selection must agree; no sprite extends into an adjacent road. Roads have a free UI draft, atomic paid confirmation and ×2 worker speed. Local zoom 50–200% and build/optional normal grid must not affect campaign/battle coordinates. Inventories use icon/count cells.
- Use delta simulation time, never wall-clock gameplay timers. All colonies tick independently of the visible screen; H21 ordinary battles run the world/economy; only their result freezes time until acknowledged (lab remains isolated). Roads change movement speed; A* lexicographically minimizes off-road distance first, then travel time; no corner cutting.
- Run `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`, then validate UI in a real desktop browser. Tests should cover campaign invariants and user flows rather than obsolete manual RTS commands.
- Keep README.md honest about balance and limitations. No engine migration, multiplayer or unrequested features.

- H8 second-tier production uses smaller attached workshops (2×2, one slot each: weaponshop/ammoshop/armorshop on factory; explosiveshop/medicineshop/psishop on chemical), placed against an owned operational factory/chemical plant (4×4, no workplaces, inventory capacity 240). Raw materials are physically carried to its inventory; recipes consume that local inventory, output enters it and supplies barracks. Default production fills storage; optional demand mode uses local stock targets. Exclusive pickup/field/gas/input-delivery claims prevent duplicate jobs. Show white/translucent workplace squares at top-left and production/storage bars below buildings.
