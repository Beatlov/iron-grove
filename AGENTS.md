# Project instructions

Desktop-mouse, single-player military space pixel strategy. User-facing text is Russian. Humans versus technological demons in space armor, on ruined industrial planets. Keep the H3 olive/sand humans, graphite/red demons, bone trim, green/amber indicators and coarse native pixel clusters.

- Offline operation and file:// support; no build tools, dependencies, remote assets/fonts or telemetry. Preview: `python3 serve.py 8000` from any cwd. Keep HTML asset query versions and visible HELIOS build stamp in sync.
- Simulation in engine.js is DOM-independent, exported through window.RTS and CommonJS. art.js must never mutate simulation. UI and scenes belong in game.js.
- H4 has 12 space regions, each a separate planet surface of 144×96 tiles. Local maps have no regional subdivision or capture outposts. Only owned surfaces may be opened; foreign planets expose status and expedition targets.
- Food, money, metal, crystals, gas, sulfur, weapons, ammo, armor, explosives and medicines physically occupy inventories of bases (5000 shared capacity), warehouses (500), and production hubs (240). Team stock is an aggregate view over ready stores, excluding all reserved military supplies, never an independent duplicate ledger. Costs and upkeep use the shared total; military orders reserve every required material in real local-store inventory until pickup. Carriers must retain loads when local stores are full. Captures transfer inventories; destroyed stores lose goods. Population is global: residents + military/training + six reserved places per colony mission. Do not duplicate counts when units travel or enter battle.
- Residents are humans, never manually commanded, automatically build/repair/transport. Hire them for 20 money at a base in a sequential 8-second queue (max 5), reserving population capacity immediately. Staffing is controlled by building cards. Stationary workers hide indoors; farmers sow fields (0.6 worker-seconds), grow crops (6 seconds), harvest exactly 4 per cycle (0.6 seconds) and process for 0.4 seconds. No new sowing until growth and all harvesting are finished; early harvest must preserve the fixed cycle yield. No automatic births or converting residents into soldiers.
- Gas and sulfur are collected at finite sources with unlimited travel distance from their respective stations (2 assigned collectors); place stations at least 4 center-to-center tiles from matching sources, returned and sealed there, then transported as ordinary goods. Raw loads never enter storage directly. Ore and gas are conditionally tier 1 and weapons tier 2; tiers are metadata only, without special visuals or logistics. Weapons recipe: 8 metal + 2 gas. Mines must cover matching ore deposits and only extract within their footprint.
- Military units exist in campaign rosters, not settlement entity lists. Train using class-specific supplies (weapons/ammo/armor; medicines/armor; explosives/armor), money and capacity. Training waits for physical delivery of all resources. Fleets carry chosen counts. Battles have independent 48×32 fields, automatic attacks/healing/kiting, no manual orders. Supply shortages slowly remove individual military units.
- Unguarded worlds are captured after travel; default neutral worlds require a confirmed battle victory. Colonization costs resources and six free slots, then creates a base and six new residents. Enemy victory transfers damaged buildings and part of the civilian population, preserving surviving workplace assignments. Residents repair automatically for metal.
- H6 uses exact grid-aligned footprints: 4×4 base/factory/barracks, 3×3 house/warehouse/farm/guild, 2×2 extraction/field/tower. Rendering, collision, preview and selection must agree; no sprite extends into an adjacent road. Roads have a free UI draft, atomic paid confirmation and ×2 worker speed. Local zoom 50–200% and build/optional normal grid must not affect campaign/battle coordinates. Inventories use icon/count cells.
- Use delta simulation time, never wall-clock gameplay timers. All colonies tick independently of the visible screen; H13 battles pause campaign time until the result is acknowledged. Roads change movement speed; A* lexicographically minimizes off-road distance first, then travel time; no corner cutting.
- Run `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`, then validate UI in a real desktop browser. Tests should cover campaign invariants and user flows rather than obsolete manual RTS commands.
- Keep README.md honest about balance and limitations. No engine migration, multiplayer or unrequested features.

- H8 second-tier production uses smaller attached workshops (2×2, one slot each: weaponshop, ammoshop, armorshop, explosiveshop on factory; medicineshop on chemical), placed against an owned operational factory/chemical plant (4×4, no workplaces, inventory capacity 240). Raw materials are physically carried to its inventory; recipes consume that local inventory, output enters it and supplies barracks. Default production fills storage; optional demand mode uses local stock targets. Exclusive pickup/field/gas/input-delivery claims prevent duplicate jobs. Show white/translucent workplace squares at top-left and production/storage bars below buildings.

## H13 neutral encounters (current rules)

Default campaign still has no strategic opponent/AI, but eleven neutral planets have 2–4 NPC guards (xenos, robots, demons, pirates), stored in region.guardians, never civilian entities or player population. Each sector also has two guarded reward sites. Arrivals wait for user confirmation; until then the campaign runs. Combat and its unacknowledged result freeze ALL campaign timers/economy/missions/upkeep. `combat.js` installs the current encounter/battle methods on Game; browser order engine → combat → legacy art → art → game; Node engine imports combat automatically. Sites grant rewards once (overflow to existing pending refund ledger), then block expeditions for 1200–1799 campaign seconds before a new generation. No strategic NPC expansion. Five trainable classes include psyker; preserve physical supply for its mixed weapon/medicine/armor cost. Damage types, grenade AoE, medic mental immunity and survivor XP live in combat.js. ## H15 structure management

Player-owned structure cards support paid-site cancellation (full cost refund), confirmed demolition (no refund for finished construction, but preserve inventories/output/unfinished recipe materials through existing refund/pending ledger), and free instantaneous same-planet relocation. Last ready command core is protected from demolition. Factories move/remove with their attached workshops atomically; standalone workshops can reattach to a valid compatible host without duplicate recipes. Preserve IDs, health, queues, inventories, staffing, crop/production/construction progress on movement; apply normal geometry/road/worker/resource-source restrictions without paying a second construction cost. Clear/rebuild paths after geometry changes. Demolition cancels training/hiring, returns their paid money/delivered supplies, releases source allocations and indoor workers, and never duplicates in-transit loads. Combat destruction remains separate and may lose store contents. No edits during battle/result pause. Road drafts track an explicit last cell (not Map insertion order); a fresh pointerdown on that cell confirms the complete valid draft exactly once. Document refund/relocation rules in help and guide.

## H16 dense placement

Buildings, fields and workshops may touch edges/corners. Replace the old one-cell blanket clearance with at least one FULL side whose directly adjacent cells are walkable empty ground and/or roads; deposits, structures, impassable terrain and map boundary block access. Partial free cells along an otherwise blocked side do not qualify; diagonal touching does not block a side. Construction must preserve access of existing edge-adjacent structures as well as its own. Movement uses the same rule, checking an entire projected factory/workshop compound and affected neighbours together, after ignoring its old positions. `hasFreeSide`/`layoutAccessValid` are DOM-independent engine rules, shared by preview and commit. Retain overlap, road, civilian, ore/collector/host/recipe restrictions. This is local edge access, not a global flood-fill/connectivity requirement.

## H17 road removal

Road tool shows its mode controls even with an empty draft. «Снос дорог» switches to an unpaid orange removal selection; «Строить дороги» returns to construction, clearing the draft. Confirm via button/Enter/fresh click of last draft cell; cancel via Esc/right click. `roadRemovalPlan` validates complete input/ownership/campaign state then filters to existing roads; `removeRoads` deletes only those cells atomically, with no resource cost or refund. Empty cells/duplicates do not delete extra roads. No deletion during active combat or result pause, or after campaign end. Clear worker routes/retry timers but preserve jobs and cargo; removed road becomes walkable normal-speed ground. Never use road removal to delete entities.

## H19 isolated battle lab

Main menu «Тест боя» creates a separate Game through battle-lab.js, using the exact campaign startBattle/battleTick implementation. Never swap or mutate the campaign instance to run lab matches. Config supports human classes on the left and human/NPC classes on the right, per-type ranks, uint32 seed, 1–30 units per side; full initial HP from unitProfile. Copy/validate config before session creation. Repeats create a fresh session from its original config, without damage/XP carryover. battle-lab-ui.js owns menu/config/result controls and its own pause/speed/fixed 0.05s accumulator; game.js routes frames/keys while it is open. Initial-menu access must work without started=true, and returning to an existing campaign must preserve simulation/UI state and restore normal battle controls. Browser loading order includes battle-lab.js after combat and battle-lab-ui.js after game; CommonJS engine installs battle-lab after combat. No persistence after page close.
